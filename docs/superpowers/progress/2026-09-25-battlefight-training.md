# BattleFight 3v3 training progress (2026-09-25)

## Implemented and verified

- Isolated headless Taro/Planck worker with six bot participants, three per team, and no HTTP/network listener.
- Training roster generated from real selection scripts: 44 main selectable characters, including Rhythm Assassin, Engineer, Emo & Sky, and Casker. Custom Unit and all other unfinished/scriptless characters remain excluded.
- Team hostility, once-only team scoring, bot-only respawn, per-life/per-character statistics, actual health-delta damage, and wall-bounce statistics. Summons do not count as match participants.
- Separate detached supervisor with 1-8 workers, `train:3v3`, `train:stop`, `train:status`, `train:export`; results and heuristic evaluation checkpoint persist under ignored `taro-engine/training-data/`.
- Heuristic candidate versus champion paired self-play with side swapping and validation bootstrap. Auto-update defaults off; `train:auto`, `train:activate`, and `train:rollback` manage normal-game policy selection at match start.
- Real projectile script resolution and one-wall ricochet aiming, restricted to projectiles that physically bounce and remain alive.
- Python 3.14.7 project venv with torch 2.14.0+cpu and NumPy 2.5.3; neural observation/action mask, PyTorch MLP, Node inference, and cross-runtime golden-vector check. Online candidate-only trajectory collection, PPO optimization, versioned weights/optimizer checkpointing, paired champion/archived validation, and a promotion gate are connected. Neural auto-activation remains off.
- Virtual timer primitive and a training-only manual-step bridge exist. `--speed max` is opt-in and enabled only after a short realtime/max trace-parity preflight; a failed preflight falls back to realtime and remains visible in status.
